A few answers

Before you jump in.

Are campaign prices fixed?

No. Both parties discuss and agree on pricing, deliverables and terms before a campaign is confirmed.

Can anyone start a chat?

Private chat is available to approved business and creator accounts, with the other participant's profile ID.

Does PromoBridge process payments?

Payments are not enabled yet. Do not treat an enquiry or campaign discussion as a completed booking.

Are campaign results guaranteed?

No. Reach, views, sales and enquiries vary by audience, content and platform.
