The important details

Clear from the start.

Is PromoBridge only for large creators?

The platform is intended for creators and businesses to connect around relevant collaborations. Fit and campaign terms should be discussed directly.

Are prices fixed?

No. Pricing, deliverables and timelines are agreed between both parties before a campaign is confirmed.

Does the portal process payments?

Payments are not enabled yet. A profile, message or campaign discussion is not a completed booking or payment.

Are views or sales guaranteed?

No. Performance depends on content, audience, platform distribution and many other factors.
