NO SURPRISES. JUST STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Know what to expect.

Can emerging creators join?

Yes. PromoBridge is designed for creators at different stages. Businesses and creators should discuss audience fit, deliverables and campaign terms directly.

Are prices fixed?

No. Pricing, deliverables and timelines are agreed between both parties before a campaign is confirmed.

Can I pay or withdraw through PromoBridge today?

Not yet. Payment checkout, wallet top-ups and withdrawals are not live. A profile, message or campaign discussion is not a completed booking or payment.

Are views or sales guaranteed?

No. Performance depends on content, audience, platform distribution and many other factors.
